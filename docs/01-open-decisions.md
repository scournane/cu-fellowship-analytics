## Still open

Five terms remain undefined. Each one currently withholds a measure. None of
them is blocking the work that does not depend on them — the participation
report runs today.

### 1. Performance
*Blocks: `performance_index`, `cohort_performance_trend`*

Participation is defined; performance is named separately in the scope of work
but has not been. Is performance distinct from participation, and if so what
evidence constitutes it — assignment quality, project milestones, staff
assessment?

Note: this is why the report shows participation *points* and a component
breakdown rather than a single 0–100 score. A normalised score would be an
implicit definition of performance.

### 2. Falling behind
*Blocks: `at_risk_flag`, `intervention_queue`*

What threshold, over what window, against what baseline — cohort median, a
fixed bar, or a Fellow's own prior weeks?

Deliberately not assumed. The first threshold that ships becomes the standard
the moment someone reads the dashboard and acts on it.

The report does surface the raw observation this would rest on: a Fellow who
is enrolled and has no recorded signal in a week is shown as "no recorded
signal", in neutral type. What it will not do is call that *falling behind*.

### 3. Fellow support responder
*Blocks: `intervention_queue`*

Confirmed on 10 August that the role does not exist yet and is expected as the
team expands.

Until it does, intervention features stay off. A flag with no assigned
responder produces a list of labelled people and no support, which is worse
than producing nothing.

### 4. Retention
*Blocks: `retention_enforcement`*

How long is per-Fellow behavioural data kept, and what happens at expiry —
deletion, or aggregation into cohort-level counts?

This one has a dependency worth flagging: year-over-year cohort comparison is
in the scope of work, so *some* aggregate has to outlive the raw record. The
retention decision and the year-over-year feature have to be made together.

### 5. Correction process
*Blocks: `correction_log`*

If a record is wrong — a missed Zoom check-in, an assignment submitted by
another route — who can correct it, through what route, and is the correction
audited?

The store is append-only today, so corrections currently require a re-ingest.
That is fine, but it is not a process.

## How a decision gets applied

Editing `config/definitions.toml`, not code:

```toml
[terms.falling_behind]
status      = "confirmed"
owner       = "Adiah Price-Tucker, Director of Programs"
decided_on  = "2026-09-01"
source      = "check-in, 2026-09-01"
definition  = """
No recorded signal from any source for two consecutive enrolled weeks.
"""
```

Re-run `make report`. The withheld panel loses that entry and the measure
appears. The definition, its owner and its date are printed in the report from
then on.
